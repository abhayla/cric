Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

CricScores ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CricScores mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Phone Number

We collect your phone number solely for account authentication. CricScores uses phone OTP (one-time password) verification as the only sign-in method. We do not collect passwords, email addresses, or any other personal identifiers during registration.

Match and Cricket Data

When you use CricScores to score cricket matches, we collect and store:

  • Match details (teams, players, overs, format)
  • Ball-by-ball scoring data (deliveries, runs, wickets)
  • Scorecards, batting and bowling statistics
  • Team rosters and player names you create
  • Tournament configurations and results

This data is necessary to provide the core functionality of the App.

Automatically Collected Information

We do not collect device identifiers, location data, contacts, or usage analytics beyond what is necessary for the App to function.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your identity via phone OTP verification
  • Store and display your cricket match data
  • Enable live scoring and real-time spectator views
  • Sync your offline scoring data to our servers
  • Provide team management and tournament features

3. Data Storage and Security

Your match data is stored locally on your device (offline-first) and synced to our servers located in India. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data during transmission and storage.

Match data is transmitted over secure connections and stored in a PostgreSQL database on our server infrastructure in India.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service:

  • Firebase Authentication (by Google) - Used solely for phone number OTP verification. When you sign in, your phone number is processed by Firebase to send and verify the OTP code. Firebase's handling of your data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising services, analytics SDKs, or other third-party tracking tools.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your match data may be visible to other users only when you choose to make a match publicly viewable through the live spectator feature.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Match data and statistics are kept to provide you with historical records and career statistics.

If you request account deletion, we will delete your account and associated personal data (phone number) within 30 days. Match data that has been part of shared matches may be retained in anonymized form to preserve the integrity of other users' records.

7. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us at support@cricscores.in. Upon receiving your request, we will:

  • Delete your account and phone number from our systems
  • Remove your personal association with match data
  • Complete the deletion process within 30 days

8. Children's Privacy

CricScores is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@cricscores.in, and we will promptly delete such information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
