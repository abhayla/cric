## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
CricScores is a free cricket scoring app built for amateur cricketers in India. Score your gully cricket, tennis ball, or leather ball matches with the same professional ball-by-ball scoring used in international cricket.

LIVE BALL-BY-BALL SCORING
Tap to record every delivery — runs, extras, wickets, and more. The 10-step scoring engine handles all the complex cricket rules automatically: free hits after no-balls, strike rotation, maiden overs, wide and no-ball extras, and all 11 dismissal types including run outs, stumpings, and caught & bowled.

OFFLINE-FIRST — SCORE ANYWHERE
No internet at the ground? No problem. CricScores stores all scoring data locally on your phone. Score your entire match offline and it syncs automatically when you're back online. Never lose a single delivery.

FULL DETAILED SCORECARDS
Get complete scorecards after every match:
- Batting stats: runs, balls faced, 4s, 6s, strike rate
- Bowling stats: overs, maidens, runs conceded, wickets, economy
- Fall of wickets with partnership details
- Over-by-over run progression

TEAM MANAGEMENT
Create your teams and manage player rosters. Add players with their phone numbers and positions. Your teams and squads are saved and ready for every match — no re-entering names.

TOURNAMENT MANAGEMENT
Organize your local tournaments with built-in support for:
- Round-robin group stages
- Knockout brackets
- Points tables with net run rate
- Automatic match scheduling

LIVE SPECTATOR VIEW
Friends and family can watch your match live on their phones. Real-time ball-by-ball updates via WebSocket — they see every run, wicket, and over as it happens. No refreshing needed.

ALL FORMATS SUPPORTED
- T20 (20 overs)
- ODI (50 overs)
- Custom formats: 1 to 50 overs
- Configurable wide and no-ball penalty runs
- Super Over for tied matches

CAREER STATISTICS
Track player performance across matches with career batting, bowling, and fielding statistics. See averages, strike rates, economy rates, and more — all calculated automatically.

UNDO & CORRECTIONS
Made a scoring mistake? Undo the last delivery and re-enter it correctly. CricScores keeps your scorecard accurate.

WHY CRISCORES?
- 100% free — no premium tiers, no paywalls
- No ads — ever
- No clutter — clean, focused interface
- Fast — optimized for quick scoring between deliveries
- Reliable — offline-first means your data is always safe
- Made in India, for Indian cricketers

Whether you play gully cricket with your friends, corporate cricket with your colleagues, or competitive tennis ball tournaments in your city — CricScores gives you professional-grade scoring for free.

Download CricScores and start scoring your next match.
```
